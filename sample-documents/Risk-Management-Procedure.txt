Risk Management Procedure
Document ID: QP-008
Version: 1.5
Effective Date: 2024-01-15

1. Purpose
To establish a systematic approach for risk management throughout the product lifecycle in accordance with ISO 14971:2019 for medical devices.

2. Scope
This procedure applies to all medical devices developed, manufactured, and distributed by the organization. It covers risk management activities from concept through design, development, production, post-production, and decommissioning.

3. Responsibilities
3.1 Risk Management Team
- Identify hazards and hazardous situations
- Perform risk analysis and evaluation
- Implement risk control measures
- Monitor effectiveness of risk controls

3.2 Design Team
- Incorporate risk management into design activities
- Implement design controls based on risk assessment
- Document design decisions related to risk

3.3 Quality Assurance
- Review and approve risk management files
- Ensure compliance with ISO 14971
- Coordinate with regulatory affairs for submissions

4. Risk Management Process
4.1 Risk Management Planning
- Define scope of risk management activities
- Assign responsibilities and authorities
- Establish criteria for risk acceptability
- Define verification activities

Risk Acceptability Criteria:
- Severity levels: Catastrophic, Critical, Moderate, Negligible
- Probability levels: Frequent, Probable, Occasional, Remote, Improbable
- Risk matrix combining severity and probability

4.2 Risk Analysis
Step 1: Identify intended use and foreseeable misuse
- Normal use conditions
- User characteristics and training
- Use environment

Step 2: Identify hazards
- Biological and chemical hazards
- Environmental hazards
- Hazards related to use or misuse
- Functional failures
- Cybersecurity threats (for connected devices)

Step 3: Estimate risks for each hazardous situation
- Severity of harm (P1)
- Probability of occurrence of harm (P2)
- Overall risk = f(P1, P2)

4.3 Risk Evaluation
- Compare estimated risks against acceptability criteria
- Determine if risk reduction is required
- Document rationale for risk acceptability

4.4 Risk Control
Hierarchy of risk control measures:
1. Inherent safety by design
2. Protective measures in the device or manufacturing process
3. Information for safety (labeling, instructions for use)

For each risk control measure:
- Verify implementation
- Validate effectiveness
- Assess introduction of new hazards
- Assess impact on overall residual risk

4.5 Residual Risk Evaluation
- Evaluate each residual risk against criteria
- Evaluate overall residual risk acceptability
- Risk-benefit analysis where applicable

4.6 Risk Management Review
- Review of risk management activities before commercial release
- Ensure risk management plan has been appropriately implemented
- Ensure overall residual risk is acceptable

4.7 Production and Post-Production Information
- Collect and review information from production
- Monitor adverse events and complaints
- Conduct trend analysis
- Review and update risk management file as needed

5. Risk Management File
The risk management file must contain:
- Risk management plan
- Hazard identification and analysis
- Risk estimation and evaluation worksheets
- Risk control measures and verification
- Residual risk evaluation
- Risk-benefit analysis
- Overall risk management review report
- Production and post-production information review

6. Integration with Design Controls
Risk management activities are integrated with design and development per QP-005:
- Design inputs informed by hazard analysis
- Design outputs include risk control measures
- Design verification confirms risk controls are implemented
- Design validation confirms risk controls are effective

7. Software Risk Management
For devices containing software (per IEC 62304):
- Software safety classification based on potential harm
- Software hazard analysis
- Software risk control measures
- Cybersecurity risk assessment

8. Biocompatibility Risk Assessment
Per ISO 10993 series:
- Identify patient contact type and duration
- Evaluate biocompatibility endpoints
- Risk management for biological hazards

9. Post-Market Surveillance
Integration with complaint handling (QP-025):
- Monitor field performance
- Identify new hazards or hazardous situations
- Update risk analysis based on post-market data
- Implement additional risk controls if needed
- Regulatory reporting (FDA MDR, EU vigilance)

10. Training
Personnel involved in risk management must complete training on:
- ISO 14971 requirements
- Company risk management procedures
- Risk analysis tools (FMEA, FTA, etc.)
- Documentation requirements
