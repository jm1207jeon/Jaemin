Quality Management System Manual
Document ID: QMS-MAN-001
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: 2024-01-01

1. Introduction
This Quality Management System (QMS) Manual describes the quality management system established and maintained by our organization for medical device manufacturing in accordance with ISO 13485:2016 and 21 CFR Part 820.

2. Scope
This QMS applies to the design, development, production, installation, and servicing of medical devices including:
- Class II medical devices
- IVD (In Vitro Diagnostic) devices
- Software as a Medical Device (SaMD)

3. Quality Policy
Our organization is committed to:
- Meeting customer requirements and applicable regulatory requirements
- Maintaining an effective quality management system
- Continual improvement of the QMS effectiveness
- Ensuring product safety and performance

4. Management Responsibility
Top management demonstrates leadership and commitment to the QMS by:
- Establishing quality policy and objectives
- Ensuring QMS requirements are integrated into business processes
- Promoting use of risk-based thinking (ISO 14971)
- Ensuring availability of resources

5. Resource Management
5.1 Human Resources
- Personnel performing work affecting product quality must be competent based on education, training, skills and experience
- Training requirements defined in procedure QP-002

5.2 Infrastructure
- Buildings, workspace, and utilities
- Process equipment (hardware and software)
- Support services

5.3 Work Environment
- Cleanroom facilities for sterile device manufacturing
- Controlled environmental conditions
- Personnel hygiene and contamination control

6. Product Realization
6.1 Design and Development
- Design planning and controls (QP-005)
- Design inputs and outputs
- Design verification and validation
- Design changes and design transfer

6.2 Risk Management
- Risk management process per ISO 14971
- Risk analysis and evaluation
- Risk control measures
- Post-market surveillance for risk management

6.3 Production and Service Provision
- Process validation (QP-010)
- Traceability requirements
- Sterilization and packaging validation
- Installation and servicing activities

7. Measurement, Analysis and Improvement
7.1 Monitoring and Measurement
- Customer satisfaction monitoring
- Internal audits (QP-015)
- Process monitoring and product measurement
- Statistical techniques

7.2 Nonconformity and Corrective Action
- Handling of nonconforming product
- CAPA (Corrective and Preventive Action) system (QP-020)
- Investigation and root cause analysis

7.3 Post-Market Surveillance
- Complaint handling (QP-025)
- Adverse event reporting to regulatory authorities (FDA, MFDS)
- Trend analysis
- Post-market clinical follow-up

8. Regulatory Compliance
8.1 FDA Requirements
- 21 CFR Part 820 (QSR)
- 21 CFR Part 803 (MDR - Medical Device Reporting)
- 510(k) submissions and PMA

8.2 European Requirements
- Medical Device Regulation (EU MDR 2017/745)
- IVDR (EU 2017/746)
- CE marking requirements

8.3 Other Regulatory Bodies
- MFDS (Korea)
- PMDA (Japan)
- TGA (Australia)

9. Document Control
All QMS documents are controlled per procedure QP-001. This includes:
- Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
- Work Instructions
- Forms and Templates
- Specifications

10. References

- ISO 14971:2019 - Application of risk management to medical devices
- IEC 62304:2006 - Medical device software lifecycle processes
- IEC 60601-1:2012 - Medical electrical equipment safety
